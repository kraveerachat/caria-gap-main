# Product

## Register

product

## Users
- SUT graduates & students trying to map digital competencies to digital careers, identify skill gaps, and explore SUT courses.
- B2B Corporate Recruiters & HR personnel searching for high-match student talent profiles and digital skill mapping.
- Academic advisors monitoring university curriculum alignment with industry demands.

## Product Purpose
- CARIA-GAP maps student self-assessment inputs against 66 competency dimensions and 78 digital career specifications, providing dynamic course recommendations to bridge the gap and showcasing aggregate market trends.

## Brand Personality
- Futuristic, premium, and professional.
- Incorporates SUT Orange (#F39200) and Nexus Blue (#2D9CFF).
- Sleek interactive 3D sphere visualization and dynamic glassmorphism highlights.

## Anti-references
- Boring corporate/clinical institutional sites that lack interactive visualizations.
- Saturated AI SaaS monoculture containing flat grays or overuse of solid card containers with side-stripes.
- Cluttered, overwhelming dashboards that dump charts without visual breathing room.

## Design Principles
- **Visual Excellence**: WOW the user with high-impact WebGL visuals and smooth micro-interactions, but maintain readable, high-contrast layouts.
- **Progressive Disclosure**: Show key match stats first, and hide detailed competency tables or simulator widgets behind interaction triggers to prevent cognitive overload.
- **Unified Bilingual Rhythm**: Maintain consistent font scaling and line heights between English labels and Thai descriptions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Contrast ratio >= 4.5:1 for body and placeholder elements.
- Accessible focus indicators and keyboard compatibility.
- Respect reduced motion settings (`@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` support).
